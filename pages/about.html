A small studio that builds the systems behind your business.

Loop&Convert is based in Zaandam, the Netherlands. We build software, automations and AI agents that take repetitive work off people, and we build products of our own.

Why we started

Most businesses we talk to don't need more software. They need the work they repeat every day to stop landing on someone's desk: the order emails, the same customer questions, the spreadsheet that gets updated by hand every Monday.

We started Loop&Convert to fix exactly that. We sit down with a business, find the job that costs the most time, and build the system that does it. Sometimes that's a dashboard, sometimes an AI agent, sometimes a full platform with logins for the whole team.

Along the way we built a product of our own, Contract Analysis, which turns a contract into a risk report in under three minutes. Building our own product keeps us sharp on what it takes to make software people rely on every day.

How we work

The problem comes first

We don't start with a tool and look for a place to use it. We start with what costs you time or money, and only build what solves it.

A fixed price, in writing

You get the scope, price and timeline before any work starts. If something changes, we agree it with you first.

Built to be used

Your team tests everything with real work before launch. If people don't use it, it isn't finished.

We stay after launch

Businesses change, and systems need to change with them. Our care plan keeps what we built running and improving.

Who you'll work with

We're small on purpose. You talk directly to the person who decides what gets built, not to an account manager.
