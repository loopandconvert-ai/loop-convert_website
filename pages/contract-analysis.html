Contract Chat

Can we get out early if the supplier keeps delivering late?

Only if the late delivery counts as a material breach (clause 13.1) and isn't fixed within 60 days. There's no specific right to terminate for repeated late delivery. Clause 8.4 gives a 2% discount per late week instead.
