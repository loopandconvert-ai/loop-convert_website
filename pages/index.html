What we build

Every project starts from a problem in your business, not from a tool. Most of what we build falls into three kinds of work.

Platforms and dashboards

Software your team and your clients actually log into. Built around how you work, not around a template.

  • Internal tools and team dashboards
  • Client portals with logins and roles
  • Back ends, databases and integrations
  • Websites that do more than show information

Automations and AI agents

The repetitive work between your tools, handled automatically, with a person stepping in where it matters.

  • Email sorting and drafted replies
  • Reading documents, invoices and forms
  • Call and chat assistants that answer and book
  • Reports and alerts sent to you automatically

Campaigns

Systems that bring in work, so growth doesn't depend on someone finding time on a Friday afternoon.

  • Outreach and follow-up systems
  • Content pipelines for social media
  • Landing pages built to convert
  • Tracking so you see what works

Built for businesses that run on people and paperwork

We work best with companies of 5 to 100 people where a few repetitive jobs quietly eat hours every week.

  • Manufacturing and workshopsJob tracking, quote requests, customer emails and planning.
  • Salons, clinics and local servicesBookings, reminders, social content and customer questions.
  • Legal and professional servicesDocument review, intake and client follow-up.
  • Webshops and wholesaleOrders, stock updates, returns and supplier emails.
