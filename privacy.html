Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy covers this website and the Contract Analysis platform.

Loop&Convert ("we", "us", "our") protects your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Dutch privacy law.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Loop&Convert, based in Zaandam, the Netherlands, is the data controller for personal data processed through this website and the Contract Analysis platform. For any privacy question, email loopandconvert@gmail.com.

3. How we use your data

We use your personal data only for the purposes listed above. Specifically:

  • To reply to questions, demo requests and call bookings.
  • To provide, operate and improve the Contract Analysis platform.
  • To send account emails, such as login links, password resets and usage alerts.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your documents or analyses to train AI models.

4. Where your data is stored

Contracts and platform data are stored and processed in the European Union. We use Supabase as our database and login provider, on EU infrastructure (AWS eu-central-1).

AI analysis is powered by the Anthropic Claude API. Contract text is sent to Anthropic for processing and is not kept by Anthropic beyond the processing of the request.

5. How long we keep data

  • Messages and demo requests from the website: up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a customer.
  • Platform data: for as long as your account is active. When you close your account, your personal data and uploaded documents are deleted within 30 days.
  • You can ask for immediate deletion at any time (see section 8).
  • Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics may be kept indefinitely.

6. Cookies and third-party content

The platform uses only essential cookies needed to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking, advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

The booking calendar on the Book a call page is provided by Cal.com and may set cookies needed for the booking to work. Fonts on this website are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads.

7. Third-party processors

We share your data only with the following processors:

  • Supabase: database, login and file storage (EU).
  • Anthropic: AI analysis of uploaded contract text (USA, under Standard Contractual Clauses).
  • Vercel: hosting of this website and platform.
  • Formspree: delivery of contact and demo form messages to our inbox.
  • Cal.com: scheduling of calls booked through this website.
  • Google: email, and fonts on this website.

8. Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the EU or EEA, you have these rights over your personal data:

  • Access: ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data.
  • Portability: ask for your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To use any of these rights, email loopandconvert@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

9. Data security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Row-level security policies on our database.
  • Access controls that limit who can see client data.

No system is completely secure. If you think your data has been compromised, contact us straight away.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We notify platform users by email before significant changes take effect. The date at the top of this page always shows the current version.

11. Contact

For any privacy question or to use your rights, email loopandconvert@gmail.com.
