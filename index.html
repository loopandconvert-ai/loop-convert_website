Huurovereenkomst_kantoorruimte_Zaandam.pdf
14 pages · analysed in 2 min 41 s · risk score 72/100
High risk
  • Rent indexation has a 3% annual floor, so rent rises even when inflation is lower.
  • Major maintenance (groot onderhoud) is moved to the tenant, departing from the ROZ general conditions.
  • The landlord excludes liability for defects, including hidden ones.
  • The lease renews automatically for five years unless notice is given twelve months ahead.

Term and renewal

art. 3

Five years from 1 January 2027, then renewed for five years. Notice period twelve months.

Rent and indexation

art. 4

€84,000 per year excl. VAT, indexed annually to CPI with a minimum of 3%.

Security

art. 6

Bank guarantee of six months' rent, callable on first demand.

Maintenance

art. 9

Tenant bears all maintenance, including major maintenance to the structure.

Subletting

art. 11

Not permitted without prior written consent. No standard of reasonableness for a refusal.

High

Indexation floor of 3% (art. 4.2) moves the inflation risk entirely to the tenant.

High

Landlord excludes liability for hidden defects (art. 8.4). Check against art. 7:209 BW.

Medium

Major maintenance is on the tenant (art. 9.1), departing from the ROZ default.

Medium

Twelve months' notice before automatic renewal (art. 3.2) is easy to miss.

  1. Will the landlord accept a cap on CPI indexation instead of a 3% floor, or drop the floor altogether?
  2. Can major maintenance return to the landlord, as in the ROZ general conditions?
  3. Will the landlord add that consent to sublet may not be unreasonably withheld?
